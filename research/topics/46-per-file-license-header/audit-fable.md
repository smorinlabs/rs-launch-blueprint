## Principle and agreement level

The decision states the shared requirement as: one unambiguous machine-readable licence declaration per distributable artifact, the root carrying the texts that declaration names, and no source file carrying a marker that can contradict it (`:25`). It sets the agreement level at **policy**, with the mechanism free to vary per ecosystem (`:27`, `:29-32`).

I concur, and the level is the right one under spec §2's table rather than a convenient one. What is shared here is a deliberately shared *value* — the owner-fixed `license` parameter `MIT OR Apache-2.0` (`docs/port/PARAMETERS.md:10`, fixed 2026-09-02) — plus a protocol detail: that the package manifest declaration and the root texts, not any file comment, are authoritative. Spec §2 assigns exactly that shape to "Policy or mechanism", where only aspects outside the agreement may vary. The decision then places the per-file marker outside the agreement and says so explicitly in the "may vary" cell (`:32`). That is the disputable move, and it is argued rather than asserted: Cargo is the first-party distribution authority and defines `license` as an SPDX expression; the Rust API Guidelines (C-PERMISSIVE) prescribe that expression plus `LICENSE-APACHE` and `LICENSE-MIT` at the root (`:34`). Both statements were independently checked by the Terra evidence check and returned **ok** (`raw/evidence-terra-r4.md:16`). A per-ecosystem difference resting on the ecosystem's own first-party guidance is precisely the justification spec §2 requires.

Two boundary tests confirm the level is not set low to make the recommendation easy. First, the decision keeps a hard invariant where agreement genuinely binds: *every* workspace package must resolve the same expression, not merely the root manifest (`:31` item 1, `:38`). That is stricter than what the sources do and was added because an executed control showed a member silently declaring `GPL-3.0-only` while the root was untouched (`review/evidence/audit-per-file-license-header-r2.log`, cited at `:160`; I confirmed the r3 reproduction in the log). Second, it does not weaken the "no contradicting marker" clause down to what any script it had could enforce — the arrow of the five revisions runs the other way, and revision 6 keeps the full policy while handing enforcement to P03.

The departure from the pre-A5 framing is disclosed rather than buried: `DIVERGENCE-ANALYSIS.md:174` called ts's D-024(7) one-line SPDX intent "already the org's decided answer" with `harmonize: yes`, and `OWNER-REVIEW.md:87` records `accept`, codesign `b`.
