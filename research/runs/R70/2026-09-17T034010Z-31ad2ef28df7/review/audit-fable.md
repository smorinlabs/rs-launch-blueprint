The untested half is the other side of the split: `From<JsonRejection>` routes `rejection.body_text()` into `logging_only(...)` and `log_detail()` exists to read it back, but no criterion calls `log_detail`, so "the rejection text is logged" is implemented and unasserted. One assertion would close it.

## Assessment

The recommendation follows from the evidence as written, and **it does not move under any finding, blocking or not**.
