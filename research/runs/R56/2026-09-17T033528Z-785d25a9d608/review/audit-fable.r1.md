## Findings

**JF-1 — blocking. Acceptance criterion 4 marks the chain's middle rung as executed, and no assertion establishes it.** The criterion reads: "`--token-file` outranks a set `RSLBP_TOKEN`, which outranks the stored credential; an empty-string env value is treated as unset (F242 parity). **executed** (`C1`, `C3`)."
