# Contributor License Agreement (CLA) FAQ

## What is a Contributor License Agreement (CLA)?

A Contributor License Agreement (CLA) is a legal document in which you confirm
that you are entitled to contribute the code or documentation you submit and
grant the project rights to use and redistribute that contribution.

## Why do I need to sign a CLA?

Signing a CLA protects both contributors and maintainers by documenting clear
copyright and patent grants alongside the repository's dual `MIT OR Apache-2.0`
license.

## How do I sign the CLA?

When CLA Assistant is enabled on a repository, opening a pull request triggers
a status check with a link to sign once via GitHub:

- **Individual contributors:** review the [Individual CLA](cla/individual-cla.md).
- **Corporate contributors:** review the [Corporate CLA](cla/corporate-cla.md).

## Do I need to sign for every contribution?

No. You sign once per repository, and CLA Assistant remembers your signature
for future pull requests.
