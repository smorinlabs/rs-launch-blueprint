How to read a row: **Principle** is the intended engineering outcome;
**Agreement level** is one of `capability/standard`, `architectural pattern`
or `policy/mechanism` (spec §2 table); **Classification** is `retained` (fit
argument recorded, no research item re-decides it), `challenged` (a
`BASELINE-REVIEW:` finding follows), or `blocked pending R##` (fitness cannot
be settled until that item decides its architecture; the row stays inherited
as a lead, not a requirement). Owner-fixed parameters were not challengeable.

Five workers reviewed one batch each; a separate adversarial reviewer from a
different model family sampled the result before reconciliation. The
reconciliation applied to the ledger, prompts and dependencies is recorded in
the `## Reconciliation` section; classification history is preserved in the
ledger rows themselves.
